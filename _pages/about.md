I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of California, Santa Cruz.

My research interests include large language model (LLM) systems, LLM serving, and AI infrastructure. I am particularly interested in improving the efficiency and fairness of systems that serve large language models.

I am currently exploring research problems related to LLM serving and scheduling.
