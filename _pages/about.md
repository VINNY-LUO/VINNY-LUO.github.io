I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of California, Santa Cruz (UCSC), where I work with [Prof. Chenguang Wang](https://cgraywang.github.io/).

I am broadly interested in natural language processing and machine learning, particularly in large language models. I am currently exploring research problems in these areas.

Before joining UCSC, I received my bachelor's degree from the Department of Electronic Engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in 2026. During my undergraduate studies, I was fortunate to be mentored by [Prof. Chen Chen](https://chenc10.github.io/).
